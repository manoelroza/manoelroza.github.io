Ancient East • Ireland

Hook Head Lighthouse

Shining bright for over 800 years on the rugged southeastern coast of Ireland.

Guided Lighthouse Tours

Step back in time with an immersive guided tour inside one of the oldest operational lighthouses in the world. Originally constructed by the Knight William Marshal in the 13th century, these ancient stone walls carry centuries of fascinating maritime stories, medieval history, and dramatic tales of shipwrecks along Ireland’s treacherous coastline.

Climb the 115 spiral steps to the top balcony where you will encounter interactive exhibits and historical re-enactments. Reaching the lantern room rewards visitors with an unforgettable experience of standing inside a living piece of world history that still guides ships safely home today.

Spectacular Coastal Views & Wildlife

Perched dramatically at the tip of the Hook Peninsula, Hook Head offers breathtaking panoramic vistas across the vast Atlantic Ocean and the surrounding sea paths. The limestone headland provides stunning natural vantage points where giant waves crash against ancient carboniferous rock formations.

The waters surrounding Hook Head are a thriving sanctuary for marine wildlife. Visitors regularly spot seals playing in the surf, while during winter months, humpback whales and fin whales are frequently sighted feeding close to shore alongside dolphins, porpoises, and sea birds.

Visitor Centre & Maritime Café

After exploring the tower and headland, warm up at the cozy Hook Head Visitor Centre and Maritime Café. Enjoy fresh, locally sourced dishes, traditional Irish baking, clam chowder, and freshly brewed coffees while taking in panoramic views of the ocean directly from your dining table.
